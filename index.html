Explore the field shapes in M. N. Wilson’s Superconducting Magnets, chapter 3, pp. 20–40.

Educational models only. Not validated engineering design or a safety assessment.

Make a prediction, adjust a winding, compare the fields, then explain and try a fresh case. Mistakes cost nothing. All sections are open.

B is magnetic field, measured in tesla (T). J is current per winding area. NI is turns × current. The bore is the useful empty opening.

Progress records completed exercises, not a certification of mastery. It is saved in this browser when storage is available.
